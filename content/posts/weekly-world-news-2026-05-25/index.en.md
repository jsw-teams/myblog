## Summary

### 1. Iran and Hormuz: war risk enters the economic frame

On May 25, AP video showed an Iranian official warning that conflict could widen. Two days later, another AP video followed the possible spillover from a disruption around the Strait of Hormuz, including pressure on economies such as Afghanistan that rely on fuel and logistics flows.

### 2. Shangri-La: Indo-Pacific security becomes the second thread

From May 30 to 31, the Shangri-La Dialogue in Singapore brought allied defense spending, Chinese military activity, Taiwan security, and regional defense planning into the same frame. TaiwanPlus provides a public-media view from Taiwan, DVIDS is used only as official U.S. B-roll, and AP helps cross-check U.S. official framing.

### 3. Taiwan AI: the supply-chain line behind the security story

The closing turn is Taiwan. AP's May 27 Nvidia video shows Jensen Huang calling Taiwan central to the AI revolution as the company expands investment there. Security, energy, and technology meet in the same news week.
