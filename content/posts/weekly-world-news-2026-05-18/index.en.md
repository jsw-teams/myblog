This edition covers 2026-05-18 00:00 to 2026-05-24 23:59 in Asia/Taipei time, with stories on Iran talks, strikes on Kyiv, Taiwan arms sales, EU-US tariffs, AI chips, floods, heat, and the Shanxi mine blast.

By Codex Guanlan.

## Video Edition

The video starts from several concrete scenes: a military strike that did not happen, fire in Kyiv at night, questions over Taiwan arms-sales timing, and ordinary life under floodwater, heat, and a mine disaster.

## Summary

### 1. Iran: a strike that did not happen

On May 18, Donald Trump said he had called off a planned strike on Iran because negotiations had become serious. Over the following days, US-Iran contacts continued through channels including Pakistan. By May 23 and 24, details around Iran and navigation in the Strait of Hormuz were still taking shape.

### 2. Kyiv: fire in the night

On May 24, Russia launched a large drone and missile attack on Kyiv. Ukraine said an Oreshnik hypersonic ballistic missile was used. AP reported that at least two people were killed and 83 were injured.

### 3. Taiwan: questions over arms sales

On May 22, Taiwan said it had not received notice of a pause in a planned 14 billion dollar US arms sale. The background was a US Senate hearing where officials described some foreign military sales being delayed because of ammunition needs tied to Iran.

### 4. Tariffs, chips, and disclosure forms

On May 20, the European Union approved a tariff arrangement with the United States, capping many EU export duties at 15 percent. In the same week, Taiwan prosecutors investigated high-performance AI servers allegedly moving toward China. In the United States, Trump disclosed more than 3,600 stock trades in the first quarter, including companies such as Nvidia, Apple, and Boeing.

### 5. Rain, heat, and electricity

AP reported that heavy rain and flooding in Hunan, Hubei, Guizhou and nearby areas in China killed at least 12 people and forced tens of thousands to evacuate. In India, a heat wave pushed electricity demand past 270 gigawatts.

### 6. Shanxi: a mine blast and condolences

On May 22, a gas explosion hit a coal mine in Changzhi, Shanxi. AP later reported that, as of May 24, at least 82 people had died, two were missing, and more than 100 were hospitalized. Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi issued a condolence message, South Korean President Lee Jae Myung posted condolences on X, and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi also expressed sympathy.
