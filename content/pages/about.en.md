Blog.js.gripe is a place for technical practice, troubleshooting notes, and long-running writing.

Posts here try to keep the shape of the problem, the reasoning around it, and the final outcome close together. The point is not just to arrive at an answer, but to leave a useful path behind.

## Privacy notice
