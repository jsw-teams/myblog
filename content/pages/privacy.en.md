Blog.openjsu.com is a public static blog. It currently uses no analytics, advertising, marketing tracking, or comment service. It does not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## Browser-local data

The site stores only language and privacy choices in your browser's local storage. The site server does not read these values. You can change or withdraw them at any time through “Privacy settings” in the footer, or delete them by clearing this site's browser data.

## Necessary request information

The server and upstream network providers may process request information needed for security, troubleshooting, and content delivery, such as IP address, request time, path, and browser identifier. The site does not use this information for advertising profiles.

## Optional third-party features

If analytics, marketing, or another optional third-party feature is added later, it will load only after explicit consent for its category, and this policy will be updated. When the browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, marketing and cross-context sharing remain disabled.

## Access, correction, and deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact the site maintainer through the [project repository](https://github.com/jsw-teams/myblog). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public request.
